## Interview Prep Notes

Bank Guarantees foundation to research before interview:

- A bank guarantee is a promise by a bank to pay a beneficiary if the customer fails to meet obligations under an agreement.
- Common use cases include property leases, construction contracts, trade obligations, performance/security guarantees, and replacing cash deposits.
- Product concerns likely include issuance, amendment, cancellation, customer experience, operational controls, risk assessment, document quality, fees/pricing, regulatory obligations, and remediation.

Suggested answer if asked about direct Bank Guarantees experience:

I have not owned a Bank Guarantees product directly, and I would be transparent about that. What I bring is senior ownership of complex enterprise products, finance workflow automation, risk-aware decisioning, and AI-enabled process simplification. I would ramp quickly on Bank Guarantees by learning the product mechanics, customer journeys, operational pain points, control obligations, and portfolio metrics, then use that understanding to prioritise improvements with Product, Operations, Technology, Risk and frontline teams.
